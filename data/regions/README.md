# Kazakhstan weather stations

English · [Қазақша](README.kk.md) · [Русский](README.ru.md) · [简体中文](README.zh.md)

The regional cooling and heat-recovery studies use 21 hourly weather files from the Climate.OneBuilding 2011–2025 TMYx collection compiled by Linda K. Lawrie and Drury B. Crawley. Each typical year has 8,760 temperatures. It joins twelve representative months, so two cities’ Julys can come from different years. The records support comparisons of seasonal operation. Equipment sizing also requires local design conditions. [Kazakhstan weather catalogue](https://climate.onebuilding.org/WMO_Region_2_Asia/KAZ_Kazakhstan/index.html) · [TMYx sources and methods](https://climate.onebuilding.org/sources/default.html)

The source-year count and period below come from the individual EPW header. Taraz Airport has 10 source years within 2011–2021 and Aktau has 14 within 2011–2025. The count describes the records available when the compiler selected the representative months. Three files are based on ERA5 reanalysis, a gridded reconstruction of weather. The other files identify NCEI ISD/ERA5 as their source. The catalogue uses Kapchagay for Qonaev, Uralsk Airport for Oral and Ust-Kamenogorsk Airport for Oskemen.

The manifest gives the EPW coordinates, elevation and UTC offset, the source-year count and period, the catalogue period, and SHA-256 digests for the ZIP, EPW and CSV. The extracts retain local standard-time hour indices 1 through 24 and the original year label for each row. The Astana entry uses the reference CSV in the parent directory. The monthly exports include the source metadata, as does the annual comparison for all cities. [Station files and checksums](stations.json)

## Reproduce an extract

Download the station ZIP using its `source_url` in the manifest, unpack the EPW and use the existing parser from the project root. For example, the following command reproduces the Almaty extract.

```bash
python weather.py --epw KAZ_ALM_Almaty.Intl.AP.368720_TMYx.2011-2025.epw --output almaty_check.csv
```

**Almaty station check.** The city comparison uses the airport at 680.9 m. The former city-centre file (851 m) averaged 19.7 °C in its selected July 2014, against 24.9 °C in the same station’s daily summaries. The airport file averages 24.8 °C for its selected July 2022, against 26.1 °C in airport daily summaries. Its June 2024 mean of 21.2 °C is also below the observed 24.3 °C. These checks compare each file with its own station and source month. The airport data are used as distributed. Local terrain, the choice of station and the remaining warm-season discrepancy affect the interpretation of cooling results. [Former city-centre weather archive](https://climate.onebuilding.org/WMO_Region_2_Asia/KAZ_Kazakhstan/ALA_Almaty_City/KAZ_ALA_Almaty.368700_TMYx.2011-2025.zip) · [NOAA city observations, 2014](https://noaa-gsod-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/2014/36870099999.csv) · [NOAA airport observations, 2022](https://noaa-gsod-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/2022/36872099999.csv) · [NOAA airport hourly records, 2024](https://noaa-global-hourly-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/2024/36872099999.csv)

The parser checks the full ordered calendar and dry-bulb range. Compare the SHA-256 values of the archive, EPW and extracted CSV to the three digests in the manifest. The technology workbench pairs these five-column records with matching hourly humidity, station pressure and global horizontal irradiation.
