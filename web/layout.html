Plant assumptions

Weather input

The model reads 8,760 outdoor dry-bulb temperatures from the Astana typical year. Applying the control rule hour by hour counts cold nights and warm afternoons separately.

Cooling sources and assumptions · Astana hourly weather CSV

How outdoor air takes up heat

The IT equipment warms the circulating coolant. The return stream enters the outdoor dry cooler first. The chiller removes the remaining heat and the pump circulates the coolant back to the IT equipment.

Operating limits at supply

What the three percentages measure

Chiller-off hours count complete hours of outdoor cooling. The dry-cooling heat share also includes heat removed during assisted hours. Electricity savings compare the two plants after counting chiller, fan and pump input.

The worked hours show how the same equipment responds to cold, mild and warm outdoor air.
